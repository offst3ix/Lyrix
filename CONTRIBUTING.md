# Contributing

Thanks for your interest in Lyrix!

**Lyrix does not accept pull requests right now.** I don't have the time to
review and maintain outside contributions, so PRs will be closed without review.

What you can do:

- **Found a bug or have an idea?** Tell me on the [St3ix Discord](https://discord.gg/6NQe7SaZtC)
  (or [st3ix.net/discord](https://st3ix.net/discord) if the invite does not work).
- **Want to change something for yourself?** Fork the project. Please follow
  the license (GPL-3.0) and the additional terms in [NOTICE.md](NOTICE.md):
  keep the credit and give your fork its own name.
